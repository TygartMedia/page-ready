What is AI Visibility for Local Service Brands?

AI visibility is how often answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity cite your brand when a buyer asks a category question. Tygart Media measures it with citation checks, entity mapping, and referral signals.

Why AI visibility matters in 2026

Buyers ask AI before they call. If your competitors are in the answer and you are not, the phone never rings.

How do you improve AI visibility?

Ship entity-clear pages, FAQ schema, and primary facts agents can quote.
